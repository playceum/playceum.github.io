FEATURES

What makes it cutting-edge?

In Playceum's Minecraft division, you launch Minecraft: Java Edition from our own client. Here are the four things inside.

READY

Press play. It's all set.

Performance mods, shaders, maps, building tools. The things most players install are there from the start. Pick how you want to play and press the button.

  • Mod set, settings and key bindings come ready-made
  • No need to install Java yourself
  • Settings you change are never overwritten

LEARN

Play it, and see how it works.

Comes with game modes made for learning. Capture what you built in the game and get a reading of it back.

  • Redstone: capture a circuit and get its diagram plus things to watch for
  • Building: reads your material mix, depth and lighting
  • Python: write and run code in an in-game editor
  • Survival: a next-step guide from your first night to the elytra
  • PvP: a HUD showing keystrokes, CPS and armor durability

13 FIELDS

Thirteen fields, all connected.

Each field has a reference, tutorials and research questions. Redstone logic gates lead to Boolean logic in programming. The fields link to each other.

  • One key opens the field guides and the Minecraft Wiki while you play
  • The redstone reference covers NOT, AND, XOR, the RS latch and more, with diagrams

MULTI

Same server, with your friends.

Start a server and you get a join code. Your friends just enter it. The required mods are installed automatically and they connect right away.

  • Start from a survival, creative, redstone or PvP template
  • Cheat mods such as X-ray are blocked
